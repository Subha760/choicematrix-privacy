Welcome to ChoiceMatrix (choicematrix.in). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how our automated bot and social publishing services interact with your platforms.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ChoiceMatrix ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our automated posting bot, social integration services, applications, and website. Our services interact with third-party social platforms including Meta (Facebook, Instagram, and Threads), Telegram, and related APIs.

2. Information We Access and Process

In order to provide automated social media synchronization, deals broadcasting, and content publishing, our bot and application access the following data:

  • Public Profile & Account Identifiers: Basic business account identification including Facebook Page ID, Instagram Business Account ID, and Threads User ID required to route and authenticate published posts.
  • OAuth Access Tokens: Scoped, secure authorization tokens granted via Meta's OAuth dialog (e.g., pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement, instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, threads_business_basic) to perform authorized actions on your behalf.
  • Publishing Data & Content: Deal alerts, post captions, image URLs, product metadata, and links scheduled or published through our automation pipeline.
  • Technical Logs: API response codes, timestamps, and error diagnostics required for service reliability and system monitoring.

3. How We Use Information

We use the accessed information strictly to:

  • Publish automated deal posts, updates, and announcements to authorized Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts, and Threads profiles.
  • Verify authentication tokens and refresh permissions required for uninterrupted automated workflow execution.
  • Monitor rate limits and content publishing quotas (e.g., Instagram 100-post daily quota).
  • Maintain system performance, prevent spam, and resolve technical issues.

We do NOT: sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data or access tokens with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

4. Third-Party Platform Integrations

Our bot operates by connecting to third-party social network APIs. When you authorize our service, your interactions are also governed by their respective privacy terms:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Threads): Governed by Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Telegram: Governed by Telegram Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard technical safeguards to protect access tokens and credentials:

  • All API requests are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • Access tokens are securely managed with environment variables and restricted to authorized background posting services.
  • No user passwords or banking information are ever accessed or stored.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain credentials and logs only as long as necessary to provide the automated publishing service. You may revoke access or request complete deletion of any stored data at any time:

  • You can disconnect our application from your Facebook account settings via Settings & Privacy > Settings > Business Integrations.
  • For specific data deletion instructions and email requests, please see our dedicated User Data Deletion Instructions page.
