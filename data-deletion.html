User Data Deletion Policy

ChoiceMatrix respects your privacy and control over your personal data. In compliance with Meta Platform Policies and global data privacy standards, users can request the deletion of any data associated with their interaction with the ChoiceMatrix Bot and Meta App at any time.

Method 1: Remove App via Facebook / Meta Settings (Instant Self-Service)

You can remove ChoiceMatrix permissions and disconnect your account directly from Facebook at any time by following these steps:

  1. Log in to your Facebook account.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  3. In the left sidebar, navigate to Business Integrations or Apps and Websites.
  4. Locate the autopost or ChoiceMatrix application in your active integrations list.
  5. Click Remove.
  6. Check the box to delete all posts, videos, or events that ChoiceMatrix posted on your behalf if desired, then click Remove to confirm.

Removing the application immediately revokes all active OAuth tokens, preventing any further automated posting or account access.

Method 2: Request Complete Data Deletion via Email

If you would like us to permanently delete all server logs, account identifiers, and data associated with your account from our backend systems, you can submit a manual deletion request:

  1. Send an email to: help@choicematrix.in
  2. Use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request - ChoiceMatrix Bot"
  3. Include the following information in your email:
    • Your Facebook Page Name or Page ID (if applicable)
    • Your Instagram username (@handle) (if applicable)
    • The email address associated with your account

Upon receiving your request, our team will process the deletion of all associated data from our servers within 48 hours and send you a written confirmation email.
